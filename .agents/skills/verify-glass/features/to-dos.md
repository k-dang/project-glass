# To-dos

The To-dos rail area holds the user's own to-do list, kept on this device and never sent to GitHub. One card at the top adds a to-do: a title line, an optional Notes field under it, and Add in the card footer. Open to-dos follow, newest added first, so the one just added sits directly under the card. Notes under a title clamp to two lines, and a web address in them is a short link. Pasting an image into the card adds a thumbnail there; removing it drops it before Add. Each row has a checkbox named after its title, its notes beneath, thumbnails of its images, and Edit and Delete buttons. Clicking a thumbnail opens the image larger. Pasting on a row attaches another image. Edit swaps the row for Title and Notes fields with Save and Cancel, and pasting there attaches an image too. Checking a to-do moves it into a collapsed "Done (n)" section, struck through. Finished to-dos stay in the order the server sends, most recently finished first. Unchecking one puts it back among the open to-dos by when it was added, so a newer open to-do stays above it. Delete removes a to-do without asking, and its image files with it. With no open to-dos the list reads "Nothing to do.", and a failed load or save shows a "Couldn't access your to-dos" alert. A to-do mentioned in the first message of a Conversation started from it moves into Done on its own once that Conversation's PR merges, even while the list is open.

## Sub-features

- `todo-add` adds a to-do from the title input at the top of the open list; surrounding spaces on the title are trimmed and both fields clear. The Notes field is always visible. Leaving it empty adds the to-do with no notes. Notes typed there are saved with the to-do and show under its title, clamped to two lines, the same as notes added later. A web address in the notes is a short link. Enter in the title adds the to-do. In Notes, Enter inserts a line and ⌘ or Ctrl+Enter adds the to-do.
- `todo-edit` saves a changed title and notes, Cancel keeps the to-do as it was, and an unfinished edit survives switching to another area and back.
- `todo-done` checks the box and strikes through the title immediately, keeping the row in place for at least 250 ms before a successful completion moves it into Done. A failed save restores the unchecked row and shows an error. Done stays collapsed, and its rows stay in the order the server sends, most recently finished first. Unchecking a to-do in Done moves it back among the open to-dos by when it was added. A successful save confirms "To-do completed" or "To-do reopened" in a toast with the to-do's title.
- `todo-delete` removes a to-do at once.
- `todo-images` pastes images into the add card and onto an existing to-do. Thumbnails stay on the to-do, a click opens one larger, and Remove drops one. They survive a backend restart. Deleting the to-do removes the image files. An image over 32 MB shows "<name> is too big to attach" and is left out; smaller images in the same paste stay. Add creates the to-do, then saves one image per message. If one fails, the card keeps it, says "Couldn't save every image. Try again.", and Try again sends that image to the same to-do.
- `todo-done-on-merge` marks a to-do done when the PR of a Conversation started from it merges. The list updates without a reload. Ticking the to-do by hand first, then unticking it, keeps it open through a later merge.
- `todo-persist` keeps to-dos, with their notes, images, and done state, when the backend restarts.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Choose To-dos in the left icon rail.
- The add card is at the top. Type in "Add a to-do" and press Enter, or choose Add in the card footer. Notes under the title are optional. Enter there starts a new line, and the footer shows ⌘ or Ctrl+Enter, which adds the to-do. Focusing a field does not change the card's border. The new row appears directly under the card.
- Choose the pencil (Edit) or trash (Delete) on a row, or tick its checkbox.
- Expand "Done (n)" to see completed to-dos.

## Driving it with glass-verify

Preconditions:

- A fresh run; to-dos never call GitHub, so `node $V launch --github fake` avoids needing credentials. `node $V doctor` passes.

- **Empty list.** `node $V click --role link --name "To-dos"`, `node $V wait --text "Nothing to do."`, then `snapshot`/`screenshot` `todos-empty`.
- **Add (`todo-add`).** The empty page already shows `form "Add a to-do"` above the empty state, with `textbox "New to-do"` and `textbox "Notes"`. `node $V type --role textbox --name "New to-do" --text "Follow up on #42"`, `node $V type --role textbox --name "Notes" --text "After CI passes"`, `node $V click --role button --name "Add"`, `node $V wait --text "After CI passes"`. The open row shows that title and those notes, and both fields are empty. Add a second, "Delete me", with Notes left empty. The snapshot shows `list "Open to-dos"` with "Delete me" first and "Follow up on #42" under it, and only the older row has notes.
- **Edit (`todo-edit`).** `node $V click --role button --name "Edit Follow up on #42"`, `type --role textbox --name "Notes for Follow up on #42" --text "Waiting on review"`, capture `todos-editing`, then `click --role button --name "Save"` and `wait --text "Waiting on review"`. The add form's Notes field stays `textbox "Notes"`. For Cancel, open Edit again, `type --role textbox --name "Title" --text "Discarded"`, choose `button "Cancel"`, and confirm `node $V text` has no "Discarded". For an unfinished edit, open Edit, type new notes, choose Settings, then To-dos: the editor is still open with the typed notes.
- **Delete (`todo-delete`).** `node $V click --role button --name "Delete Delete me"`; `node $V text` no longer has "Delete me".
- **Done and reopen (`todo-done`).** `node $V click --role checkbox --name "Follow up on #42"`, `wait --text "To-do completed"`, and capture `todos-completed` before the toast dismisses: it names the to-do, "Done (1)" appears, and the open list reads "Nothing to do.". `node $V click --role DisclosureTriangle --name "Done (1)"` and capture `todos-done`: `list "Done to-dos"` holds the struck-through row. Tick its checkbox again, `wait --text "To-do reopened"`, and capture `todos-reopened` before the toast dismisses: the to-do is back in the open list with "Done (1)" gone.
- **Images (`todo-images`).** Focus `textbox "New to-do"` or `textbox "Notes"` and paste an image file (the helper's `type` inserts text, so over raw CDP `Runtime.evaluate` a bubbling `ClipboardEvent("paste")` on `document.activeElement` whose `DataTransfer` holds a PNG `File`). A `button "Open image 1"` and `button "Remove image 1"` appear in the add card. Remove drops it. Paste two, then Add. The open row shows `button "Open image 1 of <title>"` and `button "Open image 2 of <title>"`. Click the first: a dialog titled "Image 1" shows it larger. Escape closes it. `button "Remove image 1 of <title>"` leaves one image. Click the row's title and paste another image: it shows as the next image. `kill-backend`, wait until the reconnecting banner clears, and the thumbnails are still there. Delete the to-do and the run's `.project-glass/to-do-images` directory has no files left. A file larger than 32 MB shows a toast "<name> is too big to attach" and no thumbnail.
- **Done on merge (`todo-done-on-merge`).** Needs `node $V launch --backend fake`, whose demo model answers "Pick up the slow query PR" by switching to the branch of the demo PR "Log slow database queries". Start a New Conversation, `type --role textbox --name "Message" --text "Pick up the slow query PR"`, then choose To-dos and `click --role button --name "Start a Conversation from Reply to the review thread"`: the draft keeps the typed text with the to-do's mention after it. Pick pi (`click --role button --name "Claude Code: Opus"`, `click --role button --name "pi"`, `press --key Escape`) and `click --role button --name "Send"`. `wait --text "Done in"`, then the Conversation shows `link "Open PR #5, checks passing"`. Open it, `click --role button --name "Merge"`, `wait --text "Merged"`. Choose To-dos and stay there: once the Conversation's row reads "PR #5, merged", "Reply to the review thread" leaves the open list and the Done count goes up by one. Capture `todos-done-on-merge`.
- **Persistence (`todo-persist`).** With a to-do that has notes and one in Done, reach Relaunch as in [`app-lifecycle.md`](app-lifecycle.md)'s crash-and-relaunch recipe and `click --role button --name "Relaunch Glass"`. Once the window is back on Dashboard, choose To-dos and `wait --text "Done (1)"`. The open to-do still shows its notes; capture `todos-persisted`.
- **Proof.** Before/after snapshots and screenshots of each state above. `fake-github.log` has no to-do traffic, only the startup `GET /user`.

## Gotchas

- Glass notices a merge on its next PR list refresh, which can take up to two minutes. Wait with a long `--timeout`, not a sleep.
- Choosing To-dos to add the mention resets the New Conversation's harness to Claude Code; pick pi again before sending, since only the fake pi model runs the scripted branch switch.

- Each `launch` starts with an empty list, because every run has its own home. Prove `todo-persist` with Relaunch inside one run, not with a second `launch`.
- Relaunch opens Dashboard, not To-dos; navigate back before asserting.
- "Done (n)" starts collapsed and its rows are absent from the snapshot until it is expanded. Its accessible role is `DisclosureTriangle`, not `group`.
- `type` pastes without pressing a key, so submit with `button "Add"` and close an edit with Save or Cancel. Enter in the title, ⌘ or Ctrl+Enter in Notes, and Escape-to-cancel need `press`.
- `textbox "Notes"` is always the add form. An open editor's notes field is `textbox "Notes for <title>"`, using the to-do's saved title. `textbox "Title"` is the editor's title.
- Row buttons are named "Edit <title>" and "Delete <title>", and the checkbox is named the title alone. Choose titles that don't overlap with others in the list.
- The brief checked state needs a recording or raw CDP capture within 250 ms of the click; separate helper commands can miss it. The row waits for both the save and this feedback interval; a slow save adds no further delay.
